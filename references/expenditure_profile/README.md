# Union Budget — establishment strength and provisions (Statement 22 / Annex-7)

`establishment_strength_all_years.csv` — the Union Budget's "Estimated strength
of establishment and provisions therefor" table, parsed into one CSV across
budget years **2015-16 through 2026-27** (12 years, 1,099 rows).

Generated by `publicfinance/expenditure_profile_strength.py`. Regenerate with:

```
.venv/bin/python -m publicfinance.expenditure_profile_strength
```

Input PDFs are expected under `data/union_budget/expenditure_profile/<budget_year>/`
(gitignored; see Sources below to re-fetch).

## CAVEAT — this is STRENGTH, not sanctioned-vs-vacant

The statement reports **actual strength** (persons in position as on 1 March)
and **estimated strength** (projected for 1 March of the next two years). It
does **not** report sanctioned posts or vacancies. The actual-vs-estimated gap
is only a *proxy* for staffing shortfall; do not present it as vacancy data.
Sanctioned/filled figures come from parliamentary Q&A disclosures (separate
workstream).

## Schema

One row per ministry/department/organisation as printed in the source (plus
subtotal and grand-total rows, see below). Names are **as they appear in each
year's document** — no normalization has been applied (a shared
ministry-normalization map is owned downstream).

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `budget_year` | budget edition, e.g. `2026-27` |
| `ministry` | ministry heading as printed (equals `department_or_org` when the ministry itself is the row) |
| `department_or_org` | department/organisation as printed (roman-numeral list marker stripped) |
| `strength_actual_1mar` | actual strength in position as on 1 March of `strength_actual_year` |
| `strength_est_re_1mar` | estimated strength as on 1 March of `strength_est_re_year` |
| `strength_est_be_1mar` | estimated strength as on 1 March of `strength_est_be_year` |
| `pay_actuals_cr`, `allowances_actuals_cr`, `travel_actuals_cr` | provisions, Actuals of budget_year − 2 (₹ crore) |
| `pay_re_cr`, `allowances_re_cr`, `travel_re_cr` | provisions, Revised Estimates of budget_year − 1 (₹ crore) |
| `pay_be_cr`, `allowances_be_cr`, `travel_be_cr` | provisions, Budget Estimates of budget_year (₹ crore) |

- `strength_actual_year`, `strength_est_re_year`, `strength_est_be_year` spell
  out the 1-March reference years (budget_year − 1, budget_year, budget_year + 1;
  verified against each edition's column headers).
- "Allowances" is "Allowances (other than Travel Expenses)" in the source.
- Cells printed as `...` in the source are empty strings in the CSV.
- Rows with `department_or_org` = `GRAND TOTAL` (all years) and
  `TOTAL (excluding Ministry of Railways)` (2015-16, 2016-17 only, pre
  rail-budget merger) are totals printed in the source, kept for
  cross-checking. Filter them out for department-level analysis:
  `department_or_org` not starting with `TOTAL`/`GRAND TOTAL`.

## Method

`pdftotext -layout`, then a line state machine: entities open at ministry
headings (`N.`) or department markers (`(i)`, `(ii)` …); a row's three
strength integers and nine provision decimals may arrive on different wrapped
lines (common in 2015-16 → 2018-19); trailing page numbers printed on a
page's last body line are detected and dropped (logged per run).

## Parse quality per year

Every year passes a double cross-check: the sum of parsed department rows
equals the printed GRAND TOTAL for both `strength_actual_1mar` and
`pay_be_cr` (2015-16 pay differs by 0.01 cr — rounding in the printed total).

- **2019-20 → 2026-27** — standalone `stat22.pdf`, clean text layer, parse
  clean. Remaining logged anomalies are page furniture (garbled decorative
  header glyphs in 2023-24/2024-25, a footnote line in 2023-24 — none affect
  rows).
- **2017-18, 2018-19** — Statement 22 inside the combined `vol1.pdf`; parse
  clean.
- **2015-16, 2016-17** — Annex-7 of Expenditure Budget Vol. I inside
  `vol1.pdf`; header not repeated on every page and 2–3 lines carry trailing
  page numbers (auto-dropped, logged); grand totals reconcile. These two
  years include Ministry of Railways rows and a
  `TOTAL (excluding Ministry of Railways)` subtotal (pre-merger presentation).
